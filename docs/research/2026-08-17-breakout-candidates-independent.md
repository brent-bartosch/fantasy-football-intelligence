# 2026 Breakout Candidates — Independent Research Sweep

**Date of research:** 2026-08-17
**Draft date:** 2026-08-29
**League:** 12-team, start 2 QB, full PPR, 6pt passing TD, **+1 per rushing/receiving first down**, 0.33/rush attempt, 0.5/completion, -0.5/incompletion. Start 2RB/3WR/1TE/1FLEX. (`league_rules.md`)

**Independence note:** This pass was formed without reading any prior research in `docs/research/`. Candidate list built from scratch off August camp/preseason reporting.

## ADP baseline used

Primary anchor is **Underdog best-ball ADP as of 2026-08-17** (half-PPR, 1QB), pulled from
https://www.bestballteambuilder.com/underdog-best-ball-average-draft-position

Underdog is the sharpest publicly-visible market and updates daily, so it is the right "is this priced in?" reference. Two systematic distortions to keep in mind when translating to our league:

1. **Underdog is half-PPR.** Our league is full PPR *plus* 1 pt per first down. High-reception, chain-moving slot receivers and pass-catching backs are structurally **underpriced** by this ADP. A 90-catch/65-first-down slot WR gains roughly 45+ points on our scoring relative to a same-yardage boundary WR. This is a real, repeatable, format-specific edge — and it is invisible to every national list.
2. **Underdog is 1QB.** Per the brief, I have treated QB "values" against 1QB ADP as mostly illusory and have kept QB recommendations to a minimum (see §5).

Relevant 2026 roster churn I had to establish before evaluating anyone (all confirmed below): A.J. Brown → NE; Michael Pittman Jr. out of IND; Jaylen Waddle → DEN; Mike Evans → SF; Stefon Diggs → WAS; Wan'Dale Robinson → TEN; Kyler Murray → MIN; Isaiah Likely → NYG; David Njoku → LAC; Romeo Doubs → NE; Dontayvion Wicks → PHI; Mike McDaniel is LAC OC; Brian Daboll is TEN OC; John Harbaugh is NYG HC; Ben Johnson is CHI HC (Year 2).

## 1. Tier 1 — Highest confidence

### Josh Downs — WR, Indianapolis Colts
- **ADP:** 81.8 (Underdog, 8/17). Rising — DraftSharks moved him into their top-40 WRs on 8/15.
- **Thesis:** Michael Pittman Jr. is gone from Indianapolis and Alec Pierce is coming off ankle/heel surgery. Downs was capped at a **58.7% snap share in 2025** (down from 65.2% in 2024) and ran only **65 routes as a boundary receiver** all year; the Colts have explicitly said they are moving him outside and into more 2-WR sets. In full PPR + first downs, a high-volume between-the-numbers slot is exactly the profile half-PPR ADP undervalues.
- **Evidence:**
  - Colts.com on the plan to expand his role, incl. snap-share and boundary-route numbers: https://www.colts.com/news/josh-downs-wide-receiver-fantasy-football-usage-snap-counts-targets-2026-season
  - Colts.com camp expectations: https://www.colts.com/news/colts-have-high-expectations-for-wr-josh-downs-to-begin-training-camp
  - ESPN (Stephen Holder) — Downs "among the standout performers" in camp, could be the focal point of the passing attack: https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/49540063/indianapolis-colts-josh-downs-wide-receiver-outlook
  - DraftSharks riser note (8/15): https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-risers-and-fallers
  - FantasyPros undervalued-WR writeup citing the Pittman trade + Pierce PUP: https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/08/5-undervalued-wide-receivers-to-target-2026-fantasy-football/
- **Confidence: HIGH.** The vacancy is a transaction fact, not an opinion. The camp reporting comes from the team site *and* an independent beat writer, and the mechanism (snap-share cap removed) is concrete and measurable.
- **What would refute it:** Alec Pierce returning fully healthy for Week 1 caps the snap gain. Note a **live conflict in the reporting**: DraftSharks (8/15) says Pierce is now expected to *miss* Week 1; Yahoo (8/16) says he is *on track to play* Week 1. Also, TE Tyler Warren (ADP 62.1) is a major internal target competitor, and Daniel Jones is the QB.
- **Confirm/kill before 8/29:** (a) Colts' official Week 1 injury/depth-chart designation for Alec Pierce; (b) preseason Week 2/3 alignment — is Downs actually taking outside snaps in 11 personnel, or still slot-only?
- **Priced-in check:** Partially. He has moved up and is now a common "value" pick, so the edge is eroding — but the half-PPR ADP still does not price the first-down bonus. Worth taking at cost in our format.

### Matthew Golden — WR, Green Bay Packers
- **ADP:** 104.9 (Underdog, 8/17) — versus **Christian Watson 57.5** and **Jayden Reed 79.6**. Golden is the cheapest of the three.
- **Thesis:** Green Bay cleared out its receiver room (Romeo Doubs → NE, Dontayvion Wicks → PHI), and the Packers entered camp expecting **Golden and Watson as the boundary starters with Reed in the slot**. In the preseason opener that is exactly what happened: Golden started at X in 2-WR sets, Watson at Z, and **Reed only appeared in 3-WR sets** — meaning Golden has the larger snap floor but the smaller price tag.
- **Evidence:**
  - Yahoo / Hayden Winks preseason snap breakdown (8/16), personnel-by-personnel: https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-preseason-week-1-fantasy-football-takeaways-several-rookies-impress-offenses-offer-hints-of-usage-to-come-and-more-123715309.html
  - FantasyPros Preseason Week 1 takeaways (#3): "Featured in two-receiver sets with starters ahead of Jayden Reed": https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/08/fantasy-football-20-nfl-preseason-week-1-takeaways-2026/
  - Packers.com on Golden's camp: https://www.packers.com/news/matthew-golden-kept-the-big-plays-coming-on-family-night-aug-8-2026
  - Camp role framing (Golden + Watson boundary, Reed slot): https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fantasy-football-latest-matthew-golden-122601218.html
- **Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.** Two independent sources on the same personnel observation, plus a roster-subtraction mechanism (two WRs left the building).
- **What would refute it:** LaFleur's target distribution has historically been flat and frustrating; Golden missed time with a toe issue in camp (returned 8/11); Tucker Kraft is back from his ACL and off PUP, which restores a major target competitor.
- **Confirm/kill before 8/29:** Preseason Week 2 (8/20–8/23) personnel — does Golden stay on the field when Reed rotates off in 2-WR sets, and does he see red-zone work?
- **Priced-in check:** **NO.** Golden has not moved meaningfully and the market still prices Watson and Reed above him. This is the cleanest un-priced item on my board.

### Wan'Dale Robinson — WR, Tennessee Titans *(league-specific edge)*
- **ADP:** 111.6 (Underdog, 8/17). Essentially flat.
- **Thesis:** Robinson signed a **4-year, $70M deal with $38M guaranteed** in March 2026 to reunite with **Brian Daboll, now the Titans' OC**, and he is listed as a starter and playing the slot with the ones. He **led all WRs in slot receiving yards (622) in 2025**. His entire production profile is receptions and chain-moving — which our scoring pays twice (1 PPR + 1 per first down) and which half-PPR ADP structurally underrates.
- **Evidence:**
  - Contract and Daboll reunion: https://establishtherun.com/fantasy-fallout-wandale-robinson-signs-with-titans/ and https://www.tennesseetitans.com/team/players-roster/wan-dale-robinson/

  - Depth chart lists Ridley/Robinson/Tate as the three starters: https://www.rotowire.com/football/nfl-depth-charts/tennessee-titans-depth-chart-ten

- **Confirm/kill before 8/29:** Ward's preseason Week 2 accuracy, and Robinson's slot snap share with the ones. Also watch whether Daboll uses him on manufactured touches (which our 0.33/rush-attempt and rush-first-down bonuses also reward).
- **Priced-in check:** **NO.** He appears on essentially no national breakout list — the edge here is format arbitrage, not information.

## 2. Tier 2 — Medium confidence

### Rhamondre Stevenson — RB, New England Patriots *(ADP inversion)*
- **ADP:** 71.1 — versus **TreVeyon Henderson at 57.4**. The market has the wrong back on top.
- **Thesis:** Multiple August reports have Stevenson as the **clear lead back**, with Henderson struggling with vision and patience in camp. Josh McDaniels is the OC, and Stevenson is the only skill player left who played in McDaniels' prior Patriots system. New England also just added A.J. Brown, which should improve the box count.
- **Evidence:**
  - DraftSharks (8/15) — Stevenson riser, "clear lead back"; Henderson faller, "continued camp struggles with vision and patience": https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-risers-and-fallers
  - Boston Globe camp piece (7/31): https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/07/31/sports/rhamondre-stevenson-patriots-training-camp/
  - Patriots.com / Pats Pulpit RB previews: https://www.patriots.com/news/patriots-position-snapshot-running-backs-x2832 ; https://www.patspulpit.com/new-england-patriots-training-camp/130511/running-back-preview-2026-rhamondre-stevenson-treveyon-henderson

- **Confidence: MEDIUM.** "Vision and patience" is a scout-y, soft observation and beat consensus on backfield splits in August is frequently wrong by Week 4. But the *direction* of the market is what matters: you can buy the likely lead back 14 picks later than the likely committee back.
- **What would refute it:** Henderson is the more explosive player and the more likely receiving back; if NE is game-scripted into passing, Henderson's role is more valuable. Stevenson has fumbling history.
- **Confirm/kill before 8/29:** Preseason Week 2/3 first-team carry split and, more importantly, who is on the field on 3rd-and-5.
- **Priced-in check:** Partially — Stevenson is climbing and Henderson falling. Gap may close before your draft.

## 5. On quarterbacks in our 2-QB format

Per the brief, I have been deliberately skeptical here. Most "QB value" framing against Underdog's 1QB ADP is noise: in a 12-team 2-QB league roughly 24+ QBs are rostered, so the national discount evaporates the moment you're in your own room. Two things are *not* noise, because they are within-position discounts rather than cross-format illusions:

- **Patrick Mahomes (KC), ADP 97.3.** He is **fully cleared and a full participant with no limitations** ~8 months after ACL/LCL surgery; Andy Reid confirmed he has regained full knee strength; he is being held out of preseason purely as precaution. The market is still pricing him at roughly QB13. With 6-pt passing TDs and +0.5/completion, a healthy Mahomes is a top-5 QB in our scoring. Sources: https://www.nfl.com/news/chiefs-qb-patrick-mahomes-knee-fully-cleared-to-participate-in-training-camp ; https://www.kctv5.com/2026/08/08/patrick-mahomes-speaks-recovery-game-readiness-after-training-camp-saturday/ ; https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/football/news/chiefs-patrick-mahomes-eyeing-week-1-return-in-2026 — **Confirm/kill:** whether he takes any preseason snaps in Week 3.
- **Avoid:** Kyler Murray (open competition), Cam Ward (preseason debut was genuinely bad: 5-of-12, 57 yards, intentional grounding, dropped INT), Geno Smith (ankle), and any Cleveland QB.

One structural note: our **-0.5 per incompletion and +0.5 per completion** punishes low-accuracy, high-volume QBs far more than standard formats. That is an additional strike against Cam Ward, Bryce Young, and Anthony Richardson types, and a quiet boost for high-completion-rate passers.

## Methodological caveats

- **Preseason Week 1 only.** Preseason Week 2 runs **8/20–8/23**, when starters take their heaviest snaps of the summer. That is the single highest-value information event before your 8/29 draft — every "confirm/kill" above should be re-checked after it.
- **Snap counts from games where starters rested are close to worthless.** I have flagged this where it applies (Stribling, Houston, the Chargers).
- **Consensus is not confirmation.** Where a name appears on many fantasy sites with one underlying source (e.g., the Jayden Higgins camp-reps story), I have discounted it and said so.
- One unresolved reporting conflict: **Alec Pierce's Week 1 availability** (DraftSharks 8/15 says he misses it; Yahoo 8/16 says he's on track). This materially affects the Josh Downs case and should be resolved before drafting.
