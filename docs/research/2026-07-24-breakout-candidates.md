# 2026 Breakout Candidates — Research Pass 1

Date: 2026-07-24 · Feeds: breakout-notes layer (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-24-breakout-notes-design.md`)
Method: deep-research workflow — 5 search angles, 23 sources fetched, 111 claims extracted,
top 25 adversarially verified (3-vote refutation), 23 confirmed / 2 refuted.
Refresh pass scheduled ~Aug 20–22 (pre-freeze) to re-check ADP drift and camp checkpoints.

**How to read "our board" columns:** ADP is OUR ingested 2QB ADP; proj/tier are our
starts-weighted valuation (A′). The whole point of this layer is the *disagreement*
column — where verified real-world facts contradict what our numbers assume.

**Epistemics (agreed 2026-07-24):** consensus across outlets is not independent evidence
(shared beat-report substrate + mutual copying), and high-consensus hype gets absorbed
into ADP within weeks. Grading below therefore weights (a) verifiable facts, (b) causal
mechanism, (c) base-rate archetype — and treats consensus + rising ADP as *anti*-signal
("market already knows"). Every thesis carries a confirm/kill checkpoint resolvable
before the Aug 29 draft.

## Tier A — board-blind, mechanism-backed (the layer's real targets)

Our numbers do NOT reflect these theses. Highest note value.

### Jonathon Brooks · RB CAR · our ADP 194, proj 45, t2 · post-injury
- **Thesis:** Fully cleared by his surgeon after two ACL tears in 13 months; full camp
  participant taking first-team reps (first time in his NFL career). Carolina let Rico
  Dowdle walk and drafted zero RBs — org is signaling he's the guy.
- **Our blind spot:** proj 45 — our projection sources still price him as injured. Biggest
  fact-vs-board gap in the pool.
- **Confirm/kill:** continued full camp participation vs. any PUP/setback. Note national
  ADP already moving (125 → ~101 in July); ours (194) has not.
- Consensus: low-med (ESPN thesis; clearance corroborated by NFL.com, profootballrumors,
  July 23–24 beat reports). Verified 3-0.

### Tre' Harris · WR LAC · our ADP 205, proj 138, t4 · situation (vacated targets)
- **Thesis:** Keenan Allen's 122 targets (15 red-zone) gone; Harbaugh publicly names Harris
  a top-three receiver; locked boundary starter in year 2. McConkey takes slot work.
- **Our blind spot:** t4/proj 138 at ADP 205 — a near-free lottery ticket on 100+ vacated
  targets.
- **Confirm/kill:** **Allen re-signing kills it** — he's an unsigned FA and GM Hortiz says
  the door "is not closed." Check the week of the draft. National ADP drifting up
  (190s → 160s).
- Consensus: medium (SI + Yahoo). Verified 3-0 (merged 2 claims).

### Jayden Higgins · WR HOU · our ADP 147, proj 204, t3 · year-2 role-path
- **Thesis:** 34th overall pick 2025 (9.63 RAS), 6 TDs as rookie; Christian Kirk signed to
  SF, Tank Dell no return timetable → locked WR2 next to an injury-prone Nico Collins.
- **Confirm/kill:** Dell "crushing rehab" and could return mid-season; Collins' 2025
  absences were concussions, not soft tissue. Camp target reports confirm.
- Consensus: high (ESPN + SI + beat corroboration) — but national ADP (~130) hasn't moved
  much, and ours (147) not at all, so it's not yet priced. Verified 3-0 (merged 2).

### Xavier Worthy · WR KC · our ADP 141, proj 206, t3 · post-injury
- **Thesis:** 2025 wrecked by Week 1 torn labrum (repaired Jan 2026); entered late-July camp
  unrestricted, connecting deep with Mahomes; uncontested No. 2 behind Rashee Rice (KC's

- **Confirm/kill:** unrestricted camp usage continues; watch for a contrarian "overvalued"
  case (RotoBaller) that disputes the bargain, not the facts.
- Consensus: low-med (SI; role verified via depth charts + The Athletic). Verified 3-0.

### Chig Okonkwo · TE WAS · our ADP 161, proj 173, t2 · situation (vacated role)
- **Thesis:** Signed 3yr/$27M in March to inherit the Zach Ertz role (91 targets from
  Jayden Daniels in 2024). Deebo also gone — 171 vacated targets from WAS's top two 2025

- League-format kicker: we roster 2 TE — a late Okonkwo is a fine TE2 with startable upside.

### George Kittle · TE SF · our ADP 121, proj 210, t2 · post-injury

  Verified 3-0 (merged 3).

- Etienne to NO (296 vacated touches); every meaningful first-team offseason snap; elite
  rookie efficiency (7 TD / 93 touches, 31% MTF — 2nd among RBs); GM cited him in letting
  Etienne walk. Highest-consensus breakout in the sweep (3 outlets, 3 claims all 3-0).
- **Kill:** Chris Rodriguez Jr. (2yr/$10M) winning early-down work — beat writers call it a
  real camp battle; LeQuint Allen takes passing downs. Watch August preseason snap counts.
- Priced-in: national market still mid (RB25-ish); OUR board already has him t1/proj 304 —
  the note is conviction + the kill condition, not new valuation.

### Cam Skattebo · RB NYG · our ADP 71, proj 357, t1 · post-injury
- RB10-in-PPG rookie stretch (Wks 1–8) buried by a gruesome ankle (dislocation + fibula +
  deltoid); live 11-on-11 offseason reps, "Week 1 I'll be ready."
- **Kill:** anything short of full-contact participation in August camp — injury severity is
  the real tail risk at the price.
- Our board: proj 357 t1 at ADP 71 — already a smash value; note = injury checkpoint only.

### Emeka Egbuka · WR TB · our ADP 64, proj 276, t2 · year-2 / post-injury

  hip/groin/hamstring; target share held (23.1%, 28.9% first-read) through the fade; Evans'
  targets vacated on top.

  offense-wide, not just health.
- Our board already values him (vorp +56 at ADP 64); borderline top-60 = borderline scope.

### Kyle Pitts · TE ATL · our ADP 91, proj 214, t2 · coaching change
- Stefanski offenses: top-8 PPG TE four straight years (Njoku ×3, rookie Fannin 2025);
  Pitts tagged-then-extended 3yr/$54M. Caveat: coming off his best year — semi-obvious,
  and PPG≠totals.

### Harold Fannin · TE CLE · our ADP 80, proj 221, t2 · coaching change
- Monken's 2023–25 Baltimore offenses: #1 in TE TDs (38) and #2 in TE PPR points, layered
  on a TE8-PPG rookie year. Caveat: Ravens TE *volume* was only 11th — the edge is
  efficiency. Semi-obvious (our board already +29 vorp).

### Christian Watson · WR GB · our ADP 81, proj 239, t3 · role (red zone)
- Doubs gone, Wicks traded → ~131 vacated targets, no competition added; 34% air-yardage
  share; GB's top scoring WR after his 2025 return.
- Caveats that cap it: Reed projects the larger raw share, Kraft competes for red-zone work,
  verification split 2-1 (medium), and ADP is climbing (80s → ~63 nationally) — the edge is
  eroding in real time. Priced-in risk: HIGH.
