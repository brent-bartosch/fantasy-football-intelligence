### Draft Strategy Implications
- **1 QB (not 2):** QBs drop dramatically in value — do **not** reach for a QB. Take one mid-round starter, stream a second if needed.
- **14 teams:** Elite depth is thinner at every position; top-end talent is even more scarce. Lean into RB/WR early and hard.
- **No first-down scoring:** Removes the slot-receiver / chain-mover bonus. High-volume possession WRs lose their format edge vs. the main league.
- **6 bench + 2 IR:** Shallower bench — fewer luxury stashes, more active waiver management.
- **Enhanced DEF scoring:** Same as main — a top DEF is worth reaching for late.
